It was an old law of Oststead that the vault beneath its chapel would open only for a living person who held both the garnet ledger and the ivory seal at once.

The toll bridge at Quencombe belonged to the Brackick family, and only those born a Brackick, or presently married to one, were allowed to cross it.

By custom, the ivory locket always passed, on the death of whoever held it, to the eldest living child of that holder; no will could change this, and it passed without ceremony.
