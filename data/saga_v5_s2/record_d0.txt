It was the spring of the first year.

The Ashmere household lived in the hill farms of Mireholt. Pemor Ashmere and his wife Ellia Ashmere had 4 children, all living at home: the eldest a daughter, Vesra Ashmere; then a son, Pedan Ashmere; then a daughter, Elra Ashmere; then a son, Maric Ashmere.

Among the possessions of Rolde Fellwood was the bone seal.

Only Isdette Brackett knew that the bridge toll at Wilcombe is skimmed every month.

Only Yedette Fellwood knew that the Wexwood family once owed a blood debt to the Marroway family.

Vesra Ashmere alone carried the knowledge that a chest of silver lies buried beneath the well at Wilcombe.

Only Elrald Wexwood knew that the fire at the Fenmere granary was set on purpose.

Only Nyzek Dornick knew that the harbor master of Mireford takes bribes.

Lubin Stenoway alone carried the knowledge that the old abbey at Mirecombe hides a second cellar.

Only Elrald Wexwood knew that the Fellwood mill ledger was rewritten after the flood.

Only Eltho Brackett knew that the deed to the Mirecombe pastures was forged.

It was an old law of Mirehaven that the vault beneath its chapel would open only for a living person who held both the jade map and the oak bell at once.

The toll bridge at Wilcombe belonged to the Haskoway family, and only those born a Haskoway, or presently married to one, were allowed to cross it.

By custom, the bone lantern always passed, on the death of whoever held it, to the eldest living child of that holder; no will could change this, and it passed without ceremony.
